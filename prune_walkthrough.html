Reading prune.py

Follow the discovery of a global weight mask, from the selected images through the ranking rule to the exact surviving connections. Each of the five pruning branches has its own annotated path.

Example command

$mask = .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe prune.py --subset $subset --method imp --sparsity 0.90 --rounds 8 --discovery-updates 14080 | Select-Object -Last 1

In the same PowerShell session, $subset is the path printed by the selection example. You can also assign the full path of an existing subset.json to that variable. The example captures the resulting mask path in $mask.

Start at main() and discover(). Follow magnitude/IMP, SNIP/GraSP, or SynFlow’s synthetic pass. All five end at the survivor rule and the saved mask.
