Reading train_sparse.py

Follow a discovered mask back to its original weights, through 200 epochs on the full training partition, and into the final validation or test report. The mask stays fixed while the surviving parameters learn.

Example command

.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe train_sparse.py --mask $mask --epochs 200

In PowerShell, $mask is the mask.pt path captured by the pruning example. You can supply an existing mask path directly. Add --test when you want to evaluate a fixed configuration on the official test set.

Follow main() to the compatibility checks, weight restoration, the training loop, then the saved results. For the backward pass inside one batch, jump to the baseline’s PyTorch walkthrough.

Follow one mask through the reset

For an illustrative three-weight layer, suppose the original weights are [0.40, −0.20, 0.60], discovery ends at [0.90, 0.05, 0.70], and the discovered mask is [true, false, true]. The first final-training forward pass sees [0.40, 0.00, 0.60]. SGD can change the first and third values during final training, while the second connection’s effective value stays zero.

For the example, 200 epochs on 45,000 training images give 70,400 final optimizer updates. In validation.json, metrics.accuracy is the final fraction correct and metrics.per_class gives correct/total for each class. cost.final_seconds measures this final stage’s epoch loops, while cost.total_seconds also charges the earlier stages.
